# Data limitations and controlled release

**Status: HOLD for experimental ECRA QSAR fitting and validation.**

The analysis uses a frozen EPA ECOTOX-derived dataset rather than a freshly repeated download. The binary structure screen is provisional, not chemical identity approval. A numerical correspondence between a database value and a publication is not evidence of independent biological replication or a chemically well-defined exposure.

Original-table verification across three past papers identified 22 matching numerical rows; only 20 were trout-compatible. Original individual result tables for references 12004, 10775 and 12665 remain unauthenticated, including the 71.6/71.7 mg/L 2,4-pentanedione near-match flagged for lineage review.

The separately archived Stage76 external chemical QSAR benchmark is exploratory and cannot supply or retroactively approve the ECRA release gate. Do not represent it as validated ECRA generalization.

Required before controlled data release: correct publication PDF and page, chemical identity/form, source LC50 and original units, species/life stage/exposure conditions, concentration basis, molecular form, study lineage, independent Reviewer A/B approvals, signed adjudication, lineage-disjoint split and suitable sample coverage.
